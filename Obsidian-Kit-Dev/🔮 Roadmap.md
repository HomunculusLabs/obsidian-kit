# 🔮 Obsidian Kit Roadmap

Welcome to the Obsidian Kit roadmap! This document outlines our vision for the future of AI-powered project management and how we plan to get there. Your feedback and contributions shape this roadmap.

## 🎯 Our Vision

### Mission Statement
To create the most intelligent, intuitive, and integrated project management platform that leverages AI to help teams and individuals achieve their goals more effectively.

### Core Principles
- **AI-First Intelligence**: Harness AI to augment human capabilities
- **Knowledge Integration**: Seamlessly connect information across all tools
- **Community-Driven**: Build with and for our user community
- **Open and Extensible**: Provide flexibility and customization
- **Privacy-First**: Respect user data and privacy

### Long-term Goals (2026-2027)
- Become the leading AI-powered project management platform
- Support 1M+ active users worldwide
- Establish a thriving ecosystem of plugins and integrations
- Advance the state of AI in project management
- Enable seamless collaboration across teams and organizations
