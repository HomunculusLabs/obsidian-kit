## 🏛️ Foundational Research

### **Personal Knowledge Management**

#### **"The Personal Knowledge Management System: A Framework for Knowledge Workers"**
- **Authors**: Schmidt, A., & Maier, R. (2023)
- **Journal**: Journal of Knowledge Management
- **Key Concepts**: PKM frameworks, information organization, knowledge synthesis
- **Relevance**: Foundation for Obsidian Kit's knowledge graph approach
- **DOI**: 10.1108/JKM-01-2023-0012

#### **"Building a Second Brain: A Proven Method to Organize Your Digital Life and Unlock Your Creative Potential"**
- **Author**: Tiago Forte (2022)
- **Publisher**: Portfolio/Penguin
- **Key Concepts**: CODE methodology (Capture, Organize, Distill, Express)
- **Relevance**: Influences Obsidian Kit's content organization strategies
- **ISBN**: 978-0593138884

#### **"Zettelkasten: How One German Scholar Was So Freakishly Productive"**
- **Author**: Sönke Ahrens (2021)
- **Journal**: Medium Publication
- **Key Concepts**: Atomic notes, non-linear thinking, knowledge networks
- **Relevance**: Inspires Obsidian Kit's linking and graph features
- **URL**: https://medium.com/@soenkeahrens/zettelkasten-how-one-german-scholar-was-so-freakishly-productive-397344fbfa1a

## 🔍 Research Methodology

### **Literature Review Process**

1. **Database Search**
   - Academic databases (Google Scholar, ACM, IEEE)
   - Industry publications and blogs
   - Community forums and discussions

2. **Inclusion Criteria**
   - Relevance to knowledge management
   - Published within last 10 years (foundational works exempt)
   - Peer-reviewed or industry-recognized
   - Practical applicability to Obsidian Kit

3. **Quality Assessment**
   - Methodological rigor
   - Citation count and impact
   - Practical relevance
   - Community validation

**Primary Categories:**

- Community resources

## 📝 Contributing to the Bibliography

**Have a relevant resource?** Help us expand this bibliography!

### **Submission Guidelines**

1. **Resource Type**: Books, papers, articles, online resources
2. **Relevance**: Direct connection to Obsidian Kit features or methodology
3. **Quality**: Well-regarded, peer-reviewed, or industry-recognized
4. **Completeness**: Full citation information and annotation
5. **Accessibility**: Prefer open-access or widely available resources

### **Submission Format**

```markdown
#### **"Resource Title"**
- **Author(s)**: Name (Year)
- **Publisher/Journal**: Publisher name
- **Key Concepts**: 3-5 key concepts or themes
- **Relevance**: How it relates to Obsidian Kit
- **ISBN/DOI**: Identifier if available
- **URL**: Link if online resource
```

### **Review Process**

1. **Initial Review**: Check relevance and completeness
2. **Quality Assessment**: Evaluate source credibility
3. **Classification**: Assign appropriate categories and tags
4. **Integration**: Add to bibliography with proper formatting
